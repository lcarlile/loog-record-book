The Draft

A pick is worth the points it scored above what that draft slot normally returns at its position, so a quarterback is only ever compared with other quarterbacks. Scoring changed over the years, so points are put on a common scale and every season is centred on zero — each manager is judged against the field he actually drafted against. Busts are limited to the first three rounds, where a miss actually costs something; kickers and defences are left out of steals and busts entirely.

Luck of the Schedule

Every week, compare each manager's score against everyone that week, not just their opponent. That gives the record they deserved. The bar is what they actually got, minus what they earned — gold means the schedule was kind.
